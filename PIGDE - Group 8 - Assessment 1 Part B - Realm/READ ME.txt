These are: 	- Presentation materials 		This contains all documentary evidence used in our oral		presentation. 	- Internal communications 		With most of our team communications having occurred on Discord,		we've taken the transcripts of the important messages and collated.		them into word documents. We have also provided alternate documents.		with the original messages as screenshots for the record. This also		includes the patch notes for Realm. 	- Playtest photos 		A collation of the different photographic evidence taken throughout		development, showcasing the evolution of Realm.	- Player feedback		A collation of the feedback we received during testing and the tools		used to gather that data. 	- Research documents 		Documents as shared within the team compiling the notes from		industry and academic research used during the development of Realm. 	- Design analysis 		Internal documentation used to review the various states of Realm		throughout development. 	- Component source files 		While our tests have been analogue, there are also digital source		files for a number of components which are available in this section.Some of these sections contain further subdivisions not explained here to aid withnavigation of the provided material.Where file names refer to 'version X', this is in reference to the version of Realmthat the contents reflect. Such naming conventions are not used for file editions.
